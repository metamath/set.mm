Theorem dfprop 38459
Description: The set of sentences of propositional logic is equal to the set of sentences that either a variable encoded as a natural number, a negation of a sentence propositional logic or an implication between two sentences of propositional logic. (Contributed by Thomas van maaren, 21-Aug-2026.)

Ref Expression
